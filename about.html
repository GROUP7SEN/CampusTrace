About CampusTrace

CampusTrace is a modern, student-centric Lost & Found web platform engineered specifically for Obafemi Awolowo University, Ile-Ife.

Project Overview & Purpose

CampusTrace was conceived and developed as a software engineering school project for Group 7 SEN106/216 at Obafemi Awolowo University, Ile-Ife, led by SAMKIEL (Project Lead) and Abba's 🎀 (Assistant Lead). Misplacing items such as student ID cards, wallets, department keys, chargers, or laptops across OAU's sprawling campus frequently leads to frustration and lost belongings.

This platform replaces fragmented social media announcements with a single centralized portal where students, staff, and administrative units can rapidly post, discover, delegate, and claim lost belongings.

Key Features & Core System Capabilities

What makes CampusTrace an effective campus solution:

📢

Instant Listing Publication

Post missing or found item reports in seconds complete with high-resolution photo uploads and automated image compression.

💬

Direct WhatsApp & Email Contact

Connect finder and owner directly via pre-formatted WhatsApp chat links or direct mailto links according to the poster's chosen contact preference.

🛡️

Administrative Unit Delegation

Assign item resolution to official university bodies such as OAU Central Security, Division of Student Affairs (DSA), or Department Secretariats.

🔍

Smart Search & Filters

Filter feed listings instantly by item category (*Electronics, Student ID & Cards, Documents*), missing status, or keywords.

🔒

User Management & Moderation

Comprehensive administrator portal equipped with user account controls, suspension enforcement, password resets, and audit logs.

📱

Responsive Mobile-First UI

Fully tailored for smartphones and tablets featuring mobile card table transformations, full-screen image lightboxes, and dark theme aesthetics.

Technology Stack & Implementation

Frontend Core

Vanilla HTML5, CSS3 Custom Properties (Pure Dark Minimal Design System), and Modular ES6+ JavaScript.

Database & Cloud

Firebase Firestore Real-Time NoSQL Database for live listing updates and Firebase Storage for image assets.
